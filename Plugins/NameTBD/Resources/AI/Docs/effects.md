# Glass, shadows, foldables, flip cards, progress bars

## Glass Panel
GlassPanel (palette NameTBD) is a content widget that blurs what is behind it and draws a pane of glass over it, its child on top. Preset: Frosted (light glassmorphism), FrostedDark, Clear, Liquid (bright specular rim, inner glow), Acrylic (heavy blur, dense tint), Smoked, Tinted (blue film), ProgressiveTop / ProgressiveBottom (variable blur fading from one edge - headers over scrolling content, captions over images). Glass (FGlassLook): BlurMode Uniform / Progressive (StrongEdge, FadeStart, FadeEnd, FadeSteps), BlurStrength, Tint (FWidgetShapePaint: color, gradient, image or SVG - keep alpha 0.1-0.3), Grain, GrainSize, LightAngle (0 from the top, -45 top left), Sheen, SheenReach, EdgeWidth, EdgeColor, EdgeHighlight, EdgeBacklight, EdgeFocus, InnerGlow, InnerGlowSize, FallbackColor. Shape: CornerRadius, bFullyRounded, bIndividualCorners + CornerRadii. ContentPadding, HorizontalAlignment, VerticalAlignment. Reaction NearestButton (default: inside a Button it lights up on hover/press), OwnHover, Manual, None; HoverResponse (BlurBoost, Brighten, EdgeBoost, SheenBoost, PressDarken); Motion + EnterDuration / ExitDuration. Editing a Glass value makes Preset Custom. A glass button: the ready-made "glass" button, or a Button holding a Glass Panel.

## Drop Shadow (UI Component)
UDropShadowComponent is added to any widget as a component (not a widget in the tree). Preset: Subtle, Soft (modern card, default), Elevated, Layered (six doubling shadows - the sleek, natural one), Dramatic, Hard (unblurred offset, neo-brutalist), Glow (ShadowColor glow), Tinted (shadow in ShadowColor), Floating (oval floor shadow detached below), Contact (tight oval where it touches the floor), SoftUIRaised / SoftUIPressed (neumorphism, on mid-grey backgrounds), Inset. Layers (FDropShadowLayer: Kind Outer / Inner / Floor, Offset, Blur as CSS, Spread, Color, FloorWidth, FloorHeight), ShadowColor, Opacity, Elevation (scales offset/blur/spread). Corners MatchWidget (reads Rectangle, Glass Panel, Ellipse, rounded brushes, Background Blur, ready-made buttons) or Custom. UnderWidget Auto leaves the area under see-through widgets (Glass) clear. bReactToHover + HoverElevation, PressedElevation, HoverLift, Motion, EnterDuration, ExitDuration: cards that rise to the pointer.

## Foldable Dropdown
FoldableDropdown: named slots Header and Body. bIsExpanded, Animation (FadeSlide default, Reveal, Slide, Fade, Pop), Motion + EnterDuration (open) / ExitDuration (close), SlideDistance, bToggleOnHeaderClick, AccordionGroup (same group in the same panel: one open at a time), bFocusable. Header: HeaderPadding, HeaderColor, HeaderHoverColor, HeaderPressedColor, HeaderCornerRadius. Icon: Chevron, SideChevron, PlusMinus, Caret, Image (IconImageSource Texture/SVG, IconImage, IconSVG, IconOpenAngle), None; IconSide, IconSize, IconThickness, IconSpacing, IconColor. Body: BodyPadding, bShowDivider, DividerColor, DividerThickness. Blueprint: Set Expanded, Toggle Expanded, On Expansion Changed, On Fold Finished.

## Flip Card
FlipCard: named slots Front and Back. FlipMotion: Flip, FlipVertical, SpringFlip, LiftAndFlip, Swing (door), CoinSpin, FadeThrough, Shuffle. EnterDuration (to back), ExitDuration (to front), bReverseOnReturn, Trigger (Click default, Hover, None), Perspective, Lift, Shading, Glare, CornerRadius (round the faces' own backgrounds to match), bTiltOnHover + MaxTilt + TiltGlare (holographic tilt toward the pointer), bCastShadow, ShadowColor, ShadowBlur, ShadowOffset. Blueprint: Flip, Flip To Back, Flip To Front, Set Show Back, On Flip Started / Finished.

## Advanced Progress Bar
AdvancedProgressBar: Percent (Set Percent animates, Snap To Percent doesn't), bIndeterminate, Motion + EnterDuration (rising) / ExitDuration (falling). BarStyle: Shape Bar (Direction LeftToRight, RightToLeft, BottomToTop, TopToBottom, FromCenter; BarSize length x thickness; bFullyRounded / CornerRadius; FillEnd Rounded / Flat) or Ring (RingDiameter, RingThickness, StartAngle 0 = top, Sweep 360 or a 270 gauge from -135, bClockwise, RingCap) or Curve (fills along a line: CurveSource Template (CurveTemplate: Swoosh, Arc, SCurve, Wave, Loop, Infinity...) / Preset (CurvePreset asset) / Widget (CurveWidget: a CurveShape's name in the same Widget Blueprint); CurveSize, CurveThickness, CurveCap Round / Flat, CurveCapAngle (slanted flat ends - chevron segments), bReverseCurve); FillInset; Fill (FWidgetShapePaint; a Material paint is handed Progress 0..1, Trail and ShapeSize) with GradientSpan Track / Fill / Box; bColorByValue + ValueColors (red -> green health); Track paint, TrackOutline; Segments, SegmentGap, bWholeSegments (battery, pips); bTrail with LossColor / GainColor / TrailDelay / TrailDuration (fighting-game health); Glow, GlowSize; bShine (sweeping band); bStripes (moving diagonal stripes, bars); Markers (ticks at values). Events On Percent Changed, On Filled, On Emptied.
